## 🔗 Setting Up EasyTasks in Obsidian

EasyTasks is made to work with Obsidian’s notes and plugins. After installing it on your computer, you must activate it inside Obsidian.

Follow these steps:

1. Open Obsidian.
2. Click the **Settings** gear icon at the lower-left corner.
3. Go to **Community plugins** (you may need to turn off Safe Mode).
4. Click **Install plugin** and then choose **Manage** or **Browse**.
5. Look for `EasyTasks` in the list. If not there, click **Install from file** and select the EasyTasks plugin folder you installed.
6. After installing, enable the plugin by clicking the toggle switch.

Now, EasyTasks will appear in your Obsidian sidebar or ribbon for easy access.

## 🔄 Uninstalling EasyTasks

If you want to remove EasyTasks:

1. In Obsidian, go to **Settings > Community plugins**.
2. Find EasyTasks and click **Disable**.
3. Click **Uninstall** to remove the plugin.
4. If you installed using an `.exe`, use Windows **Add or Remove Programs** to uninstall.

Your notes and data will stay intact unless you delete them manually.
